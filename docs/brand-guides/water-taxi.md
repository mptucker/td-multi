# Texoma Water Taxi by Fastrac — Brand Style Guide & Voice

*On-demand water taxi · Island shuttle · texomawatertaxi.com · Texoma Destinations family · v1.0 · September 2026*

**Tagline:** It's like Uber. On the water.  
**Hub descriptor:** The Most Convenient

## 1. Positioning

Lake Texoma's only on-demand water taxi: $25 a person each way between Highport Marina and the islands, $100 an hour private for up to six, pickups at any marina or dock in the service area, sunrise to sunset — text and we're on the way.

**Who we're talking to.** Boaters and island-goers around Highport, restaurant-goers, slip tenants, bachelorette/wedding groups, island campers.

## 2. Personality

Quick, Casual, Reliable, No-nonsense, Helpful.

Shared Texoma Destinations attributes (2023 campaign platform): Fun, Friendly, Family, Exciting, Enjoyable, Personable, Accommodating, Experienced, Safe. USP: *The Best Lake Experience Close to Home*.

## 3. Voice

Text-message brevity. Rates, routes, phone number. The 'Uber on the water' line is the whole personality — keep the rest utilitarian and friendly.

**Do**

- Put the phone/text number in every piece.
- State rates with the qualifiers ('each way', 'from the time we leave our dock').
- List destinations by name — people search for them.
- Say 'text us' before 'book online'.

**Don't**

- Cruise-brand flourish — that's Fastrac.
- Hide surcharges (night $20, holiday $5/pp).
- Imply 24-hour service.

**Words we use:** like Uber, on the water, text us, pickup, the islands, each way, sunrise to sunset, your dock  
**Words we avoid:** cruise, experience, luxury, ferry schedule

## 5. Typography

- **Display:** Oswald (500–700)
- **Body:** Inter
- **Accent:** —

Sub-brand of Fastrac: uses the Fastrac lockup with 'Texoma Water Taxi by Fastrac' in text until a dedicated mark exists. Condensed display face = taxi-sign energy.

## 6. Logo usage

- Until a dedicated mark is designed, use the Fastrac lockup + 'Texoma Water Taxi' wordmark in Oswald.
- Yellow is the brand cue (the Mellow Yellow boat) — use it for buttons and the map pin, never for large text on white.
- Recommend: a simple taxi-check pattern + wave mark for boat livery and avatars.

## 7. Photography

- The yellow boat at a dock, people stepping aboard with coolers, island drop-offs, the service-area map.
- Bright midday, candid, phone-camera OK for social.

## 8. Key messages

- $25 per person each way, Highport ↔ the islands.
- $100 an hour private, up to six.
- Text 903.414.6050 for a ride.
- Boat drop-offs, beverage runs, island camping.
