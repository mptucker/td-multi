# Texoma Destinations — Brand Style Guide & Voice

*Hub / parent brand · texomadestinations.com · Texoma Destinations family · v1.0 · September 2026*

**Tagline:** The Most Texoma  
**Hub descriptor:** The Most Texoma

## 1. Positioning

The single place to plan and book everything on Lake Texoma — cabins, RV sites, glamping, boats, cruises, beach days and memberships — from a family of properties run by one local team. It's the best lake experience close to home, and only Texoma Destinations gives you the most of everything the lake has to offer.

**Who we're talking to.** DFW-area families and friend groups within a 90-minute drive; repeat lake-goers; boat owners; groups planning reunions, retreats and weddings.

## 2. Personality

Fun, Friendly, Family-first, Exciting, Accommodating, Experienced, Safe.

Shared Texoma Destinations attributes (2023 campaign platform): Fun, Friendly, Family, Exciting, Enjoyable, Personable, Accommodating, Experienced, Safe. USP: *The Best Lake Experience Close to Home*.

## 3. Voice

Confident host. Warm, plain-spoken, a little playful, never salesy. Talks like a local who knows every cove and wants you to have the best day possible.

**Do**

- Lead with the experience, then the logistics ('Catch a sunset, then walk to your cabin').
- Use 'we' and 'you' — we are the people on the dock, not a booking engine.
- Say the specific thing: 'Friday and Saturday nights in season', not 'select dates'.
- Superlatives are earned and local: 'the most popular beach on Texoma', 'the only year-round fuel dock'.
- Close with a clear next step: Check availability · See cruise dates · Buy a day pass.

**Don't**

- Corporate travel-speak ('accommodations', 'amenities' as a headline, 'world-class').
- Exclamation-point stacking or ALL CAPS urgency.
- Vague hedges ('may be available') — if it's not certain, don't publish it.
- Duplicating the same paragraph across properties.

**Words we use:** lake day, close to home, the most, family, cove, sunset, book, check availability, TAP members save  
**Words we avoid:** accommodations, world-class, luxurious (except Sundance), unforgettable, getaway package deal, click here

## 5. Typography

- **Display:** Big Noodle Titling (site) → web: Josefin Sans / Oswald caps
- **Body:** Source Sans 3 / Nunito
- **Accent:** Brittany Signature (script accents, sparingly)

Wordmark is a bold geometric sans with a wave under TEXOMA. Headlines set in caps display faces; body in a friendly humanist sans.

## 6. Logo usage

- Primary lockup: TEXOMA (blue) over DESTINATIONS (green) with the wave. Use the circle mark for avatars/favicons.
- Minimum width 120 px / 1 inch. Clear space = height of the 'T' on all sides.
- On photos, place on a white or navy panel — never directly on busy water.
- Never recolor the green/blue split; one-color white and navy versions exist for dark and print.

## 7. Photography

- Golden-hour water, families mid-activity (not posed), boats underway, real staff.
- Warm, slightly desaturated, horizon in the top third. Always show the lake.
- Texie the Dragon appears only in kids/family contexts.

## 8. Key messages

- One destination, more Texoma: every property, one account, one booking.
- Pay Now & Save — book online for the best rate.
- TAP: one membership, free day use, free ramps, discounts everywhere.

## 9. Sample copy

**Headline:** The best lake experience close to home.  
**Subhead:** Cabins, RV sites, glamping, boats, cruises and beach days — one lake, one booking.  
**Primary CTA:** Book your fun now  
**Social post:** Friday-night dinner cruise, Saturday tritoon, Sunday hammock. That's the Most Texoma. Dates are live — link in bio.  
**Email subject:** Your lake weekend is 90 minutes away
