# Island View Park — Brand Style Guide & Voice

*Day-use beach · Shelters · Kayak & SUP rentals · islandviewtexoma.com · Texoma Destinations family · v1.0 · September 2026*

**Tagline:** Lake Texoma's most popular beach  
**Hub descriptor:** The Most Fun

## 1. Positioning

The day-trip beach for North Texas: real sand, a swim area, covered shelters with grills, volleyball, showers and kayaks — open 9 to sunset every day, $7 to get in, kids seven and under free.

**Who we're talking to.** Day-trippers from Sherman/Denison/DFW, young families, birthday parties, church and youth groups, paddlers, TAP members.

## 2. Personality

Sunny, Punchy, Playful, Straightforward, Kid-friendly.

Shared Texoma Destinations attributes (2023 campaign platform): Fun, Friendly, Family, Exciting, Enjoyable, Personable, Accommodating, Experienced, Safe. USP: *The Best Lake Experience Close to Home*.

## 3. Voice

The lifeguard-who-isn't (we have no lifeguard, and we say so): high energy, short sentences, price and hours up front. Rules are stated plainly and end with 'have fun'.

**Do**

- Lead with price and hours — they're the whole decision.
- Use punchy fragments in headlines: 'Sand. Shade. Seven bucks.'
- Keep rules friendly and ALL-CAPS-free on the web (the sign at the gate can shout).
- Push 'buy your pass before you leave the house'.

**Don't**

- Resort language — it's a park.
- Imply there's a lifeguard or a supervised swim area.
- Bury the no-glass rule.

**Words we use:** beach day, sand, day pass, shelter, 9 to sunset, kids 7 & under free, buy ahead, paddle  
**Words we avoid:** resort, exclusive, accommodations, lifeguard on duty

## 5. Typography

- **Display:** Lilita One (rounded display)
- **Body:** Nunito (600–800)
- **Accent:** —

The logo is a playful hand-lettered 'Island View' with sun, palm and wave. Lilita One carries that bounce; Nunito keeps it legible.

## 6. Logo usage

- Lockup: 'Island View' lettering + sun/palm + wave + 'DAY USE · PICNIC AREA · BEACH ACCESS'. Drop the descriptor line below 160 px.
- Bright colors are the brand; only use the one-color black version on print forms.
- Never place on orange or yellow backgrounds — white or sky blue only.

## 7. Photography

- Sand, splashing kids, umbrellas, kayaks pulled up on the beach, shelters with a grill going. Midday bright and saturated.
- Show the crowd on holiday weekends — popularity is the pitch.
- Winter: eagles and empty sand for the 'year-round' story.

## 8. Key messages

- $7 day pass · kids 7 & under free · TAP members free.
- 9am to sunset, every day, year-round.
- Shelters $40/day, reservation only.
- Kayaks and SUPs right off the beach.

## 9. Sample copy

**Headline:** The beach day that's closer than you think.  
**Subhead:** Lake Texoma's most popular sandy beach, open every day from 9am to sunset. Seven bucks gets you in.  
**Primary CTA:** Buy a day pass  
**Social post:** Forecast says 94 and sunny. The sand says come early. Passes online — skip the gate line.  
**Email subject:** Sand. Shade. Seven bucks.
