# Fastrac Charters & Cruises — Brand Style Guide & Voice

*Public cruises · Private charters · Lake tours · Water taxi · fastrac.com · Texoma Destinations family · v1.0 · September 2026*

**Tagline:** Catch a sunset. Catch some fun.  
**Hub descriptor:** The Most Amazing

## 1. Positioning

Lake Texoma's only cruise and charter service: ticketed sunset, dinner, lake-history and themed cruises nearly year-round; private charters for two to seventy on the Island Girl, Sight-Sea-Er II and Last Call; Lake Day beach trips; and the Texoma Water Taxi. Every boat has a USCG captain.

**Who we're talking to.** Couples (date night, anniversaries), visiting friends and family, birders (eagle season), corporate groups, wedding parties, bachelorette groups.

## 2. Personality

Fun-loving, Polished, Confident captain, Hospitable, A little showy.

Shared Texoma Destinations attributes (2023 campaign platform): Fun, Friendly, Family, Exciting, Enjoyable, Personable, Accommodating, Experienced, Safe. USP: *The Best Lake Experience Close to Home*.

## 3. Voice

The cruise director with a captain's license: playful superlatives, crisp logistics. Sells the moment (sunset, eagles, murder mystery) and then nails the details (boarding 15 minutes early, BYOB, 72-hour cancellation).

**Do**

- Pair a hook with a fact: 'Texoma's largest party boat — 70 guests, climate-controlled, two decks.'
- Name the boats and give them personality.
- Use 'BYOB' proudly; it's a differentiator.
- Publish dates about a month out and say so.

**Don't**

- Overuse 'Uber' — it belongs to the Water Taxi brand.
- Mix charter and public-cruise pricing in one sentence.
- Promise wildlife or weather.

**Words we use:** catch a sunset, cruise, charter, captain & crew, BYOB, Island Girl, Sight-Sea-Er II, departs Lighthouse Marina, Texoma's only  
**Words we avoid:** boat ride (that is a specific product), yacht party, cheap, ferry

## 5. Typography

- **Display:** Josefin Sans (300–600, wide tracking) — echoes the thin geometric FASTRAC letterforms
- **Body:** Inter
- **Accent:** —

Wordmark: thin, widely spaced caps with an anchor for the T, 'CRUISES' beneath, two-tone wave. Headlines light-weight caps with generous tracking.

## 6. Logo usage

- Lockup 'FASTRAC CRUISES' with anchor + wave is primary. Legal/long form is 'Fastrac Charters & Cruises' in text.
- Boat-only square mark (anchor/boat) for avatars.
- White one-color on navy for hero overlays; never on cyan.
- Minimum width 160 px — the thin strokes disappear smaller.

## 7. Photography

- Boats underway at golden hour, guests toasting on the top deck, eagles in bare winter trees, the Island Girl lit at dusk.
- Aerials showing the wake. Clean, bright, slightly cool blues.
- Themed cruises: costumes and table settings, not stock food.

## 8. Key messages

- Texoma's only cruise & charter service.
- Sunset $35 · Dinner $60 · Fri & Sat in season.
- Private charters for 2–70; captain, crew and fuel included.
- Eagle Watching Breakfast Cruises every winter.
