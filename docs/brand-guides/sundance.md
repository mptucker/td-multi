# Sundance Camp — Brand Style Guide & Voice

*Tiny-cabin village · Group camp · Retreats · sundancetexoma.com · Texoma Destinations family · v1.0 · September 2026*

**Tagline:** Where rustic meets luxury  
**Hub descriptor:** The Most Peaceful

## 1. Positioning

Eighty private acres at the tip of Preston Point with seven themed tiny cabins, six wooded RV sites, primitive group camping and a 400-yard beach — a whole camp you take over for the people you actually want to be with. No Wi-Fi, by design.

**Who we're talking to.** Corporate retreats, family reunions, scout/church groups, intimate weddings, couples who want dark skies.

## 2. Personality

Secluded, Boutique, Grounded, Quietly luxurious, Elemental.

Shared Texoma Destinations attributes (2023 campaign platform): Fun, Friendly, Family, Exciting, Enjoyable, Personable, Accommodating, Experienced, Safe. USP: *The Best Lake Experience Close to Home*.

## 3. Voice

Calm, spare, a touch literary. Fewer words, longer pauses. Speaks to groups as hosts, to couples as confidants. The only brand that may say 'luxury' — always paired with 'rustic'.

**Do**

- Short declarative lines. Let silence do work: 'The stars. Just — the stars.'
- Name the cabins (Pecos, Glorietta, Rosa Linda, Arroyo, Pinion House, Ponderosa, Cottonwood) — they're part of the charm.
- Sell the buy-out: 'your group, your lake, nobody else'.
- Be straightforward about no Wi-Fi and the shared bathhouse — it's a feature.

**Don't**

- Party or family-fun energy — that's Lighthouse and Island View.
- Exclamation points.
- Over-decorate with adjectives; one good one per sentence.

**Words we use:** secluded, disconnected on purpose, tiny cabin village, your group, dark skies, Preston Point, chef-crafted, concierge  
**Words we avoid:** fun spot, party, kids' paradise, cheap, campground (say camp)

## 5. Typography

- **Display:** Josefin Sans (geometric caps, 600–700)
- **Body:** Lora (serif)
- **Accent:** —

Wordmark is geometric caps under a sunburst-and-tree emblem. Caps headlines, serif body = rustic-luxury contrast.

## 6. Logo usage

- Square emblem (sun + tree) over SUNDANCE CAMP is the primary mark; emblem alone for avatars.
- Works on bone/white and on charcoal; orange never used as a background behind the emblem.
- Minimum 96 px square. Do not add taglines inside the lockup.
- Legacy site had no logo of its own (used the TD network mark) — always use the Sundance emblem now.

## 7. Photography

- Blue-hour cabins with warm windows, star fields, empty beach at dawn, long tables under the pavilion, fire pits.
- Cool shadows, warm highlights; moody but not dark. Minimal people, or silhouettes.
- No inflatables, no crowds.

## 8. Key messages

- 7 cabins, 6 RV sites, 80 acres, one group.
- Sleep 50+, host 100.
- No Wi-Fi. The night sky is the entertainment.
- Chef-crafted meals and concierge extras for groups.

## 9. Sample copy

**Headline:** Where rustic meets luxury, and the lake meets the sky.  
**Subhead:** Eighty acres, seven tiny cabins, one sandy beach and no one else around.  
**Primary CTA:** Book a tiny cabin  
**Social post:** Seven cabins. One beach. Zero notifications. Sundance is open for fall retreats.  
**Email subject:** Take the whole camp
