# BoaterWise — Brand Style Guide & Voice

*On-water boating instruction (NSBC certified) · boaterwise.com · Texoma Destinations family · v1.0 · September 2026*

**Tagline:** Your course to on-water confidence  
**Hub descriptor:** The Most Confident

## 1. Positioning

Hands-on, small-group boat handling on Lake Texoma taught by NSBC-certified instructors who are also USCG captains. Four 3-hour modules to certification, private instruction on your own boat, and the Boat Club course — from the Lighthouse Marina dock.

**Who we're talking to.** New boat owners, Big Water Boat Club members, renters who want confidence, spouses/teens learning to drive the family boat, experienced boaters polishing docking.

## 2. Personality

Encouraging, Competent, Safety-first, Approachable, Lightly humorous.

Shared Texoma Destinations attributes (2023 campaign platform): Fun, Friendly, Family, Exciting, Enjoyable, Personable, Accommodating, Experienced, Safe. USP: *The Best Lake Experience Close to Home*.

## 3. Voice

A patient instructor who has seen every docking mistake and isn't judging. Clear, structured, confidence-building. Credentials stated once, then it's about you.

**Do**

- Speak to the fear directly: docking in a crosswind, launching at a busy ramp.
- Be structured: modules, hours, class size, price.
- Credentials in one line: 'NSBC-certified instructors, USCG captains'.
- Tie to the family: Boat Club members train free; TAP Navigator saves 10%.

**Don't**

- Lecture or use jargon without translating (S.C.A.N. → say what it is).
- Fear-based selling.
- Claim to be the Texas boater-education card course (it isn't).

**Words we use:** confidence, hands-on, at the helm, small group, your boat, certified, captain, module  
**Words we avoid:** school, seminar, classroom, mandatory, test

## 5. Typography

- **Display:** Exo 2 (600–800) — geometric, slightly technical like the wordmark
- **Body:** Inter
- **Accent:** —

Wordmark: bold geometric caps with the A as a sail/triangle; light-blue tagline beneath. Captain's Crest emblem for certificates.

## 6. Logo usage

- Primary: 'BoaterWise' + tagline lockup. Tagline may be dropped under 200 px.
- White-except-A version for dark headers (exists).
- Captain's Crest emblem is for certificates, instructor apparel and the 'certified' badge — not a substitute logo.
- Remove `noindex` on the site; the brand is invisible to search today.

## 7. Photography

- Student at the helm, instructor pointing, docking sequences, hands on the wheel; pontoons at the Lighthouse dock.
- Bright, clean, candid; show smiles and life jackets. No sunset glamour — this is daytime learning.

## 8. Key messages

- $199 per 3-hour module, 2–4 students.
- Instructors are NSBC certified and USCG captains.
- Complete all four modules for your Certificate of Completion.
- Private instruction on your own boat, up to 32 feet.
