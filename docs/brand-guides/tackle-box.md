# Tackle Box Outfitters — Brand Style Guide & Voice

*Bait & tackle · Licenses · Propane · Camp store · tackleboxtexoma.com · Texoma Destinations family · v1.0 · September 2026*

**Tagline:** Lake Texoma's fishing and outdoor headquarters  
**Hub descriptor:** The Most Fishing

## 1. Positioning

The last stop before the lake on SH 289: the only live-bait shop within 25 miles of Lighthouse, $12 Lake Texoma licenses, striper tackle the guides actually use, propane refills, ice, firewood and everything you forgot.

**Who we're talking to.** Anglers (local and visiting), guide clients, RV campers heading to Lighthouse/Island View, weekenders stocking a cooler.

## 2. Personality

Salty, Practical, Local, Dry humor, Knows what's biting.

Shared Texoma Destinations attributes (2023 campaign platform): Fun, Friendly, Family, Exciting, Enjoyable, Personable, Accommodating, Experienced, Safe. USP: *The Best Lake Experience Close to Home*.

## 3. Voice

The guy behind the counter: short, useful, a little gruff, secretly delighted to help. Talks in lists and tips. Never fancy.

**Do**

- Lead with what's in stock and what's biting.
- Say '$12 license, both states' every time — it's the single best fact.
- Lists over paragraphs.
- Mention Bob Jr. the bass; people love him.

**Don't**

- Retail hype ('huge selection!', 'unbeatable prices!').
- Long stories on the website — save them for the counter.
- Promise a bite.

**Words we use:** live bait, shiners, striper, license, propane, last stop, headquarters, what's biting, Bob Jr.  
**Words we avoid:** boutique, curated, premium, shop now (until the online store returns)

## 5. Typography

- **Display:** Permanent Marker (hand-lettered) — headlines only, one per page
- **Body:** Barlow (400–700)
- **Accent:** —

The logo is hand-drawn marker lettering with a red-and-white bobber for the 'o'. Keep the marker face for the H1 and use Barlow for everything else.

## 6. Logo usage

- Lockup 'the tackle box' with bobber 'o' is the only mark. Do not typeset the name in another font as a logo.
- Black on white or canvas; white one-color on black. Never on red.
- The bobber may be used alone as an icon/favicon.

## 7. Photography

- Tackle walls, bait tanks, the mounts, hands holding a striper, the storefront at dawn with trucks and trailers.
- Natural light, slightly gritty; no stock fishing photos.

## 8. Key messages

- Only live bait within 25 miles of the Lighthouse.
- $12 Lake Texoma license — residents and non-residents.
- Propane: cylinders any time, RVs by appointment.
- Book a striper guide, buy your bait here.

## 9. Sample copy

**Headline:** Everything you need for a day on the lake. One stop.  
**Subhead:** Live bait, striper tackle, fishing licenses, propane, ice, firewood and cold drinks — on Highway 289 as you head to the water.  
**Primary CTA:** Find a fishing guide  
**Social post:** Shiners in. Stripers schooling off the dam. $12 license at the counter. You know where we are.
