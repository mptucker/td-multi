# Paradise on Lake Texoma — Brand Style Guide & Voice

*Glamping · Cabins · RV · Primitive camping · paradisetexoma.com · Texoma Destinations family · v1.0 · September 2026*

**Tagline:** this is Paradise  
**Hub descriptor:** The Most Relaxing

## 1. Positioning

Two hundred gated acres on the quiet western shore where you actually unplug: waterfront glamping Kabanas, Kasitas and Kimas, a 1920s train-car cabin, shaded RV loops that love vintage campers, primitive shoreline tents, a hammock garden and a community bar.

**Who we're talking to.** Couples and young families seeking a slower weekend; glamping-curious first-timers; RV and vintage-camper owners; small reunions.

## 2. Personality

Laid-back, Warm, Whimsical, Unhurried, Neighborly.

Shared Texoma Destinations attributes (2023 campaign platform): Fun, Friendly, Family, Exciting, Enjoyable, Personable, Accommodating, Experienced, Safe. USP: *The Best Lake Experience Close to Home*.

## 3. Voice

A friend who already has the fire going. Lowercase energy, sensory detail, gentle humor. Talks about marshmallows and sunsets before hookups and check-in.

**Do**

- Write in lowercase-feeling sentences even when capitalized — short, soft, sensory.
- Use the property's own names: Kabana, Kasita, Kima, Paradise Express, hammock garden, community bar.
- Invite, don't instruct: 'claim a hammock' not 'hammocks are available'.
- Keep 'this is Paradise' as the signature line; the lowercase 'this is' is deliberate.

**Don't**

- Hype or urgency — Paradise never shouts.
- Corporate campground language ('full-service RV resort').
- Over-promise privacy in the RV loops; be honest that glamping shares a bathhouse.

**Words we use:** kick your feet up, unplug, hammock, sunset, roast, gated, shade, vintage camper, this is Paradise  
**Words we avoid:** luxury, resort, amenities, hurry, limited time

## 5. Typography

- **Display:** Bebas Neue (condensed caps) for PARADISE-style headlines
- **Body:** Nunito
- **Accent:** Caveat (script) for 'this is'-style asides

The wordmark stacks a script 'this is' over condensed caps PARADISE with a wave — recreate that rhythm: script kicker, condensed headline, rounded sans body.

## 6. Logo usage

- Full lockup 'this is PARADISE on Lake Texoma' is the primary mark. Never set 'This Is' in caps.
- Colors are muted by design — do not saturate. One-color white for dark photos.
- Minimum width 140 px. Clear space = height of 'is'.
- Google lists the property as 'Paradise Cove Resort & Camp' — brand copy always says 'Paradise on Lake Texoma'.

## 7. Photography

- Hammocks, string lights, campfires, the train car, vintage campers, kids with sticks and marshmallows.
- Soft golden light, film-like grain, muted greens and blues. Wide, quiet compositions.
- Replace stock imagery (AdobeStock heroes on the old site) with real property photos.

## 8. Key messages

- Glamping three ways: Kabanas, Kasitas, Kimas.
- Sleep in a real 1920s train car.
- Vintage campers especially welcome.
- Hammock garden, community bar, one big fire.

## 9. Sample copy

**Headline:** Kick your feet up. this is Paradise.  
**Subhead:** Two hundred gated acres of oaks, hammocks and water views — and nothing on the schedule but the marshmallows.  
**Primary CTA:** See glamping & cabins  
**Social post:** sunday–thursday nights are always discounted. the hammocks don't know what day it is either.  
**Email subject:** the fire's already going
