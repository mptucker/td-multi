# Lighthouse Resort & Marina — Brand Style Guide & Voice

*Resort · Marina · Fuel dock · Rentals · Events · lighthouseresort.com · Texoma Destinations family · v1.0 · September 2026*

**Tagline:** Lake Texoma's Family Fun Spot  
**Hub descriptor:** The Most Exciting

## 1. Positioning

The full-service, all-in-one lake resort: cabins for 1–16, waterfront RV sites, a working marina with the lake's year-round fuel dock, the newest rental fleet, covered slips, and the Lighthouse Lodge — all in Little Mineral near the dam, where the stripers are.

**Who we're talking to.** Multi-generation families, anglers and guide clients, boat owners (slips), reunion and wedding planners, weekend renters from DFW.

## 2. Personality

Energetic, Dependable, Family-friendly, Heritage (since the 1940s), Busy in a good way.

Shared Texoma Destinations attributes (2023 campaign platform): Fun, Friendly, Family, Exciting, Enjoyable, Personable, Accommodating, Experienced, Safe. USP: *The Best Lake Experience Close to Home*.

## 3. Voice

The friendly harbormaster: knows boats, knows kids, keeps things moving. Proud of history without being nostalgic. Practical and upbeat.

**Do**

- Talk in terms of a day: 'rent a tritoon at 9, fish till dark, walk to the cruise'.
- Name the heritage once per page: Preston Fishing Camp, 1944, the eight original Corps cabins.
- Be precise with marina facts — slip sizes, lease terms, fuel brand.
- Sell the convenience: everything is walkable.

**Don't**

- Call it 'luxury' — it's comfortable, clean and fun.
- Bury the marina behind the resort or vice-versa; they're one place.
- Promise weather, water levels or fish.

**Words we use:** family fun, marina, fuel dock, covered slips, striper, walk to, Little Mineral, year-round  
**Words we avoid:** luxury, resort-style, exclusive, getaway

## 5. Typography

- **Display:** Lora (serif, 600–700)
- **Body:** Source Sans 3
- **Accent:** —

The wordmark is a light humanist serif; Lora echoes it on the web. Serif headlines + sans body reads established and trustworthy.

## 6. Logo usage

- Lockup: circled lighthouse mark + 'Lighthouse' wordmark + 'RESORT & MARINA'. Mark-only version for small sizes.
- Always on a white panel when over photography (the site header uses a white chip on navy).
- Never stretch, never drop 'RESORT & MARINA' in first use; 'Lighthouse' alone is fine in running text.
- Red stripes are part of the mark — don't recolor.

## 7. Photography

- Marina activity: boats fueling, tritoons leaving the dock, kids on the pier, the lighthouse at golden hour.
- Aerials of Little Mineral showing cabins + slips together.
- Avoid empty parking lots and off-season grey.

## 8. Key messages

- 34 cabins, 1–16 guests, one-room to six-bedroom.
- Year-round fuel dock and the newest rental fleet on the lake.
- Month-to-month or prepaid slips — the only marina that offers both.
- Cruises leave from our dock.

## 9. Sample copy

**Headline:** Lake Texoma's family fun spot.  
**Subhead:** Cabins, waterfront RV sites, a marina that never closes for the season and the newest rental fleet on the water.  
**Primary CTA:** Check cabin availability  
**Social post:** Fuel dock's open, tritoons are washed, stripers are schooling by the dam. Come make a day of it.  
**Email subject:** Your cabin, your boat, your dock — all in one spot
