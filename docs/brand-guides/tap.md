# TAP — Texoma Access Package — Brand Style Guide & Voice

*Annual membership program (hub-owned) · texomadestinations.com/tap · Texoma Destinations family · v1.0 · September 2026*

**Tagline:** One pass. The whole lake.  
**Hub descriptor:** The Most Access

## 1. Positioning

One annual membership across every Texoma Destinations property: free day use at Island View and Paradise, free boat-ramp access at Lighthouse and Paradise, and 5–15% off stays, rentals, cruises, retail and fuel — Explorer $99, Adventurer $199, Navigator $399, plus the Big Water Boat Club tier.

**Who we're talking to.** Locals and lake-house owners on the Preston Peninsula, frequent day-trippers, boat owners who launch often, Boat Club prospects.

## 2. Personality

Smart, Generous, Local-insider, Clear, Rewarding.

Shared Texoma Destinations attributes (2023 campaign platform): Fun, Friendly, Family, Exciting, Enjoyable, Personable, Accommodating, Experienced, Safe. USP: *The Best Lake Experience Close to Home*.

## 3. Voice

The insider's deal explained by a friend who did the math. Benefit-led, tier-clear, never fine-print-first. Always 'members', never 'subscribers'.

**Do**

- Do the math for them: 'pays for itself in two beach days and a boat launch'.
- Use the tier names consistently: Explorer / Adventurer / Navigator / Big Water Boat Club.
- Say 'TAP' on second reference; spell out 'Texoma Access Package' once.
- Every brand site links to /tap — TAP is the connective tissue.

**Don't**

- Publish prices anywhere but the hub (they change).
- Use 'TAP Pass' as the product name (it's redundant) — 'TAP membership' or 'your TAP'.
- Carry legacy 'Individual/Family' plan language.

**Words we use:** members save, free day use, free ramp access, TAP, tier, renews annually, insider  
**Words we avoid:** subscription, TAP Pass, Annual Pass, loyalty program

## 6. Logo usage

- The TAP card graphic (navy) is the visual identifier; the hub logo remains the brand.
- Tier badges: Explorer (green), Adventurer (blue), Navigator (gold), Boat Club (navy).
- If TAP becomes its own site, keep the card + hub colors so it reads as a Texoma Destinations program.

## 7. Photography

- Members using benefits: launching at Lighthouse, walking into Island View, fueling up, boarding a cruise.
- Warm, candid, 'regulars' energy.

## 8. Key messages

- Free boat-ramp access at Lighthouse & Paradise.
- 5–15% off stays, rentals, cruises, retail and fuel.
- From $99 a year.
